## 2. Quality requirements

For each revised question:

- Preserve its stable question ID and learning objective.
- Increment its revision number.
- Archive its previous version.
- Preserve exactly one defensible correct answer.
- Ensure all four alternatives are plausible.
- Remove answer clues from assumptions and prerequisites.
- Calibrate difficulty based on actual reasoning.
- Verify the explanations for every alternative.
- Test executable examples against the pinned Laravel 13.40.0 environment and PHP 8.4/8.5 where applicable.
- Clearly identify documented-contract cases that cannot be executed locally.

Do not change the correct answer solely to alter its position.

Do not artificially equalize answer lengths with filler text.
