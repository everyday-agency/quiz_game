Pinned implementation: Illuminate/Database/Concerns/ManagesTransactions.php commits PDO before transaction-manager callbacks; Illuminate/Queue/Queue.php registers afterCommit callbacks; Illuminate/Events/Dispatcher.php defers observers implementing ShouldHandleEventsAfterCommit.
Interpretation: afterCommit is process-local ordering, not a durable queue record. Business state and pending intent must use the same connection and transaction for local atomicity. Completion before publication loses work; completion after acknowledgment permits redelivery after an ambiguous outcome. Stable event identity plus downstream deduplication handles that redelivery.
Supplementary local execution tests callback timing and atomic rows; no Redis or broker crash test is claimed.
