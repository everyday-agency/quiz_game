Exact excerpt:
"Store the files on a different server."
Paraphrase: user-uploaded active content can execute when retrieved in a browser;
storage and public delivery need their own security boundary. For the question's
download-only requirement, use an isolated origin without application credentials
and an attachment response rather than rendering untrusted HTML inline in the
authenticated application's origin. Do not claim this replaces upload validation,
malware controls or authorization. No active upload was executed in a browser.
