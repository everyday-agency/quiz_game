DUPLICATES AND ANSWER CUES

No normalized whole-question duplicate or repeated objective ID exists among
the 600 final records. The per-batch lexical scan produced 44 candidate pairs
against both the new bank and inactive legacy material. Every pair has a
recorded disposition. A second weighted whole-bank text pass produced two
candidates: 0156/0157 (single versus parent loop state) and 0193/0531 (attempt
credential/session handling versus once request-only authentication). Their
assessment objectives remain distinct.

Some standard concepts necessarily overlap the inactive legacy curriculum.
The new collection contains original stems/options/evidence; it does not
reactivate legacy records. Shared concepts alone are not proof of duplication.
Conversely, low text similarity is not proof of semantic originality.

The correct choice is uniquely longest in 146/600 (24.3%) and uniquely shortest
in 131/600 (21.8%). These aggregate signals do not provide a reliable longest-
answer rule. One ratio flag remains: 0596 has the exact output "missing" versus
short numeric outputs. It was retained rather than padded. Individual wording
and distractor quality still require independent editorial judgment.

Known related mechanisms were reviewed in context: scoped state restoration,
request-only versus persisted authentication, queued versus synchronous
invariants, transaction boundaries, stale cache fills, key rotation, retry
classification, namespace isolation and deployment compatibility. Their
framework contracts and failure observations, rather than renamed examples,
define the assessment distinction.

QUALITY LIMITATIONS

There are no known unresolved answer-key or syntax defects after the recorded
repairs and checks. This is an author conclusion, not independent approval.
In particular, no lexical metric can prove that every distractor will attract
real candidates. No empirical discrimination or item difficulty data exists.

44 documented-contract questions are explicitly non-runtime validations.
The evidence identifies each source and the limits of the inference. Small
architecture witnesses demonstrate the stated constraints, not correctness
of a complete production system. Browser tests verify presentation and
grading, not factual Laravel correctness.

Working lesson mappings are independent of identity and remain editable.
The course outline’s broad titles are not a substitute for checking the actual
lesson content before curriculum-gated examinations. No future lesson numbers
were invented.

the seven Advanced questions, framework-version-sensitive behaviors, and
the remaining full collection. Record independent decisions separately from
this completed internal review. Preserve revisions for any later correction.
